# 01 — Vision & Mission

## One Line Pitch
> *Jarvis for traders. Always live, always watching, speaks before you ask.*

---

## What TradingBrew IS
- A live AI trading companion — always on, always aware
- A voice-native journal that logs trades without the trader feeling like they're journaling
- A discipline and psychology coach that knows the trader deeply over time
- A proactive agent that speaks first when necessary
- A relationship — the buddy no trader has ever had
- A trader's personalised newspaper (X posts, feuds, achievements, market moments)
- A pre/post market briefing system
- A rule enforcement and prop firm safety net

## What TradingBrew is NOT
- Not a signals provider — ever
- Not a charting platform
- Not a broker or execution tool
- Not a backtesting engine
- Not financial advice — legally critical
- Not a competitor to TradingView, Tradovate, Rithmic, or MT4/MT5
- Not a surveillance tool — never creepy, always warm

---

## The Core Philosophy
> Most traders don't lose because they lack a strategy.
> They lose because they break their own rules.
> TradingBrew is the buddy that stops that from happening —
> not by judging, but by knowing.

---

## The Positioning
```
TradingView  → charts, data, execution
TradingBrew  → psychology, discipline, relationship
```
Traders say: *"I use TradingView for charts, TradingBrew for everything else."*
That sentence is the entire marketing strategy.

---

## The Vision (3 years)
- Daily AI performance coach for every serious trader globally
- So deeply personal that switching feels like losing a mentor
- Acquired by a major prop firm or trading platform for $10M+
- The standard tool every prop firm recommends to their traders

---

## The Soul
After 6 months of daily use, a trader should feel:
> *"This thing knows me better than my trading mentor,
> my friends, and honestly sometimes myself."*

That feeling = zero churn. Ever.
