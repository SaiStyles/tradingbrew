# 02 — User Personas

## The Universal TradingBrew User
```
Any trader who:
- Trades actively (daily or weekly)
- Has broken their own rules before
- Wants to be more disciplined
- Hangs out on X or Discord
- Pays for tools that genuinely help
```

---

## Primary Target — Prop Firm Trader
- Age: 22-35
- On their 2nd or 3rd funded account attempt
- Knows their strategy works but keeps breaking rules
- Follows trading accounts on X obsessively
- Has tried Notion journal, abandoned it in week 2
- Biggest fear: blowing another account emotionally
- Platforms: Apex, TopStep, FTMO
- Will pay anything that genuinely helps

## Secondary Target — Retail Futures Trader
- Age: 28-40
- Self funded, trades NQ or ES
- Profitable some months, destroys it other months
- Knows psychology is the issue
- Wants data on himself, not the market
- Will pay for clarity and accountability

## Tertiary Target — Forex Trader
- Age: 20-35
- Highly active on X/Twitter
- Community driven, shares trades publicly
- Influenced heavily by who they follow
- Will try anything their favorite trader recommends
- Largest volume, most viral potential

---

## Priority Order
```
1st → Prop firm traders (highest pain, fastest pay)
2nd → Futures retail traders (high value, daily users)
3rd → Forex traders (biggest volume, most viral)
```

---

## Where They Hang Out
- X/Twitter — discovery, viral moments, trader drama
- Discord — daily community hangout, word of mouth
- YouTube — consumption only, not community

---

## Their Pain Points
- Breaking rules they set themselves
- No memory of past mistakes patterns
- Journaling feels like homework
- No one to talk to after a bad trade
- Blown accounts from emotional decisions
- No awareness of their own psychology patterns
