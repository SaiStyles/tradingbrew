# 10 — Go To Market

## Launch Strategy

### Phase 1 — Soft Launch (Month 1-2)
- Give access to 10-20 trader friends personally
- Get brutal honest feedback
- Fix everything broken
- Do not publicise yet

### Phase 2 — Community Launch (Month 3)
- Post in relevant Discord trading servers
- Share on X/Twitter with demo video
- Target prop firm communities specifically:
  - Apex Trader Funding Discord
  - TopStep community
  - FTMO trader groups
  - Futures trading Discords

### Phase 3 — Influencer Outreach (Month 4-5)
- Identify 5-10 trading educators on YouTube/X
- Offer free Pro access forever
- Ask for honest review not paid shill
- One genuine endorsement = 500+ users overnight

### Phase 4 — Organic Growth (Ongoing)
- Let the product sell itself
- Every user who loves it tells their trading Discord
- Word of mouth in tight trading communities is powerful
